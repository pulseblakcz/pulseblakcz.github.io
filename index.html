Privacy Policy — PULSE Last updated: 10 August 2026 PULSE ("the game") is developed and published by Batuhan (contact: newhomenewlifes@gmail.com). This policy explains what the game does and does not do with your data. What the game does not collect PULSE has no user accounts, no login, and no servers of its own. It does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, location, or any other personal information. Your scores, your unlocked skins, your daily streak and your settings are stored only on your own device and are never uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the game deletes all of it. Advertising PULSE shows optional rewarded video ads: after a run ends you may choose to watch a short advert to continue playing. You are never required to watch one, and no part of the game is locked behind an advert. These adverts are served by Google AdMob. To show them, AdMob collects your device's advertising identifier and shares it with Google. This identifier is used for advertising and marketing purposes, including measuring adverts and detecting invalid traffic. Google's own privacy policy explains how it handles that data: https://policies.google.com/privacy, and how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time from your Android settings, under Settings → Privacy → Ads. Doing so does not stop you from playing the game. Consent in the European Economic Area If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the game shows you a consent form the first time it runs, provided by Google's User Messaging Platform. You choose there whether adverts may be personalised. You can change that choice later from the same form. Children PULSE is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from children. Changes If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new date at the top.
